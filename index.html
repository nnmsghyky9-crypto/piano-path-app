            Help: microphone & keyboard connection

            Microphone (works on iPhone): the page has to be opened over https://. If you blocked the mic, go to iOS Settings → Safari → Microphone, or tap ᴀA in the address bar and choose Website Settings. Allow it, then reload. Keep the phone near the piano's speakers and don't cover the mic with your hand.

            MIDI over USB or Bluetooth: Web MIDI works in Chrome or Edge on desktop and in Chrome on Android. Plug the keyboard's USB-to-Host port into the computer; Android needs a USB-OTG adapter. Then tap Connect keyboard. A keyboard you plug in later is found automatically.

            iPhone / iPad: Safari, and every other iOS browser, does not support Web MIDI, so on iOS use the microphone.
